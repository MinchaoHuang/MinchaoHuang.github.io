Research Interests

I am interested in LLM reasoning, interpretability, and reinforcement learning. I want to understand how language models reason and what post-training actually changes inside them, and to use that understanding to train models that reason more reliably. Recently, I've been working on model diffing in natural language: using reinforcement learning to train a prompt generator that reveals what fine-tuning changed in a model.

I'm also excited about applying LLMs to real-world problems.

PragReST: Self-Reinforcing Counterfactual Reasoning for Pragmatic Language Understanding
Minchao Huang*, Jihyung Park*, Leqi Liu, Elias Stengel-Eskin
Findings of EMNLP, 2026
arXiv / code

The model generates its own pragmatic QA data and counterfactual reasoning traces, then learns from them with SFT and GRPO, with no human-labeled training data.

Minchao Huang*, R. Zhou*, Leqi Liu
Under review

A prompt generator trained with GRPO surfaces, in natural language, the behaviors that fine-tuning changed in a model.

Exp-Force: Experience-Conditioned Pre-Grasp Force Selection with Vision-Language Models
Minchao Huang*, S. Shang*, B. Fan, L. Chin
Under review
arXiv / project page

Retrieves similar past grasps and lets a vision-language model reason over their measured forces to choose a grasp force for a new object from a single image.

Deep Learning-Based Detection of Cognitive Impairment from Passive Smartphone Sensing with Routine-Aware Augmentation and Demographic Personalization

IEEE BHI, 2025
